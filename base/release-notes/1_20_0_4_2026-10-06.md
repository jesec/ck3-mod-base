# 1.20.0.4

**Release Date:** 2026-10-06
**Official Announcement:** https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/1158310/view/684140727200909402

---

Hello everyone! Our 1.20.1 update is still in active development, and many of the issues you’ve reported will be fixed or improved in that release. In the meantime, we’re rolling out a small hotfix today to address a high priority issue that was preventing players from educating their children.


Check out the changelog below for more details!


---


### Update 1.20.0.4 Changelog

-   Fixed a bug where the secondary recipient of an interaction would become unavailable after using an artifact interaction, preventing interactions such as educating your children.


---


As always, if you encounter any issues after today's update, please ensure you have disabled _all_ mods and file a report [via our Bug Report forum](https://steamcommunity.com/linkfilter/?u=https%3A%2F%2Fforum.paradoxplaza.com%2Fforum%2Fforums%2Fcrusader-kings-iii-bug-report.1143%2F) if the issue persists.
