# 1.21.0.3

**Release Date:** 2026-11-19
**Official Announcement:** https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/1158310/view/699907769355993654

---

Hello everyone! We're pushing a small hotfix today that fixes several issues. It's small in scope but should address one of the biggest pieces of feedback from you in the community.


This hotfix is compatible with existing 1.21 saves and mods. Check the changelog below for more information!


---


# Update 1.21.0.3 Changelog

-   Restored starting domain limit grace period and made it prioritize disabling less important holdings and districts
-   Added levies to villages, walls, manor houses and military district main buildings
-   Manor Tower now gives levies
-   Lessened the administrative/celestial/meritocratic penalty for levies to 25% (from 50%)
-   Halved impact on levies from Persian struggle
-   Theodosian Walls now give raw levies instead of a multiplier
-   Fixed the game getting paused by an invisible Guelphs & Ghibellines intro event
-   Reduced the gold cost of title creation and usurpation
-   Prevented lieges without treasury from gaining a ton of gold by revoking titles from their republic baron vassals
